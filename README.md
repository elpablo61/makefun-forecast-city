One line, at most 120 characters: the funniest weather forecast for a city that does not exist.

> Sleetford: snow, -6°C. Mr. Abernathy shovelled his drive at dawn. The snow waited till he finished, then came back.

`main.ts` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`).
